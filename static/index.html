Trade-level policy

Forced signal + grade

FORCE_SIGNAL=true (default): always BUY or SELL, graded A–D by how much was read from the chart vs estimated. FORCE_SIGNAL=false: strict mode, returns WAIT unless both AIs agree and price/ATR are verified. Mark each signal TP1 / TP2 / SL in Signal History to see your real win rate.
ATR formula

Transparent and deterministic

Effective ATR = 0.65 × primary ATR + 0.35 × secondary ATR scaled by the square root of primary timeframe minutes / secondary timeframe minutes. SL = 1.20 × effective ATR; TP1 = 1.00R; TP2 = 1.80R. R equals the SL distance. This is a configurable risk convention, not a guarantee of profitability.

Gemini free-tier availability, quotas and models can change. DeepSeek image-analysis access may depend on your API account and selected model. Keep model names configurable in .env. The application does not ship API keys, a database password, or sample/fake signal data.
